A calm VR scene where you stand at the end of a wooden dock and watch the sun set over a lake. You can look around and lean over the edge of the dock, and the Touch controllers show up in your hands.

The scene is made from Unity primitives and standard URP materials:

- **Dock:** cube planks and cylinder posts, with a glowing lantern (emissive sphere + point light)
- **Lake:** a large plane with a smooth, reflective material
- **Sky:** Unity's Procedural Skybox tuned for an orange sunset, a low directional light and linear fog
- **Scenery:** autumn trees (cylinder trunks + sphere leaves), and hills and mountains made from flattened spheres
- **VR:** `OVRCameraRig` with controller models

Everything is generated by one editor script, `Assets/LakeDock/Editor/LakeDockBuilder.cs`, which adds the menu item **Tools > Lake Dock > Build Scene**. The scene is saved to `Assets/LakeDock/LakeDock.unity` and set as the first scene in the build.
